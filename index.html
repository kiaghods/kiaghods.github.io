Hi! I'm a (rising) senior at Princeton studying computer science and mathematics. I think about machine learning, systems, robotics, and how machines and people learn.

My research as an undergraduate has mostly been in the space of machine learning, (1) its intersections with cognitive science (under Professors Jon Cohen and Tom Griffiths), and (2) using theory as the backbone of better empirical methods (under Professor Elad Hazan).

Outside of this, I aim to make my peers (and myself) more excited about computing and its applications in our world. As such, I help run Princeton ACM and Gradient.
